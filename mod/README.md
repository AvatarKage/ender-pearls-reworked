Ender Pearls Reworked gives you complete control over your Ender Pearls, letting you decide exactly when and where to teleport. No more worrying about unsafe destinations or accidentally plunging to the bottom of the ocean.

With this mod, throwing an Ender Pearl doesn’t move you automatically. Instead, you physically vanish from the world and oversee the pearl’s entire live travel route. While teleporting, shift-clicking or interacting with an item or the environment will instantly consume the pearl, releasing you back into the world at that exact location. This gives you precise control over your movements. Use it to gain height for mace attacks, escape danger, or travel without ever touching the ground.

Compatible with [Skippy Pearls](https://modrinth.com/datapack/skippy-pearls) and [Enderman Overhaul](https://modrinth.com/mod/enderman-overhaul).
